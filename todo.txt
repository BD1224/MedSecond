Description: This project (MedSecond) is a web platform where users can securely upload medical images and descriptions of their health concerns to receive human-reviewed opinions from verified medical professionals and students, rather than AI. It focuses on accessibility and affordability by offering a streamlined way to obtain second opinions, while ensuring trust through a verification process for contributors based on their credentials or education status. Medical reviewers can build reputation through their activity and feedback, and users benefit from multiple perspectives on their condition in a centralized, easy-to-use dashboard. The platform aims to bridge the gap between expensive traditional second-opinion services and impersonal AI tools by providing real, community-driven medical insight in a safe and structured environment.
