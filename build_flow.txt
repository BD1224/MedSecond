MEDSECOND IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP (CRITICAL PATH FIRST)

PHASE 1: FOUNDATION & IDENTITY (THE "WHO")
Goal: Setup the data layer and basic entry into the app.
1.  db/tables/users.sql (User schema & roles)
2.  db/tables/patient_profiles.sql (Patient health basics)
3.  db/tables/assessor_profiles.sql (Assessor professional basics)
4.  src/lib/db.ts (Database connection utility)
5.  src/lib/auth.ts (Logic for handling sessions/JWTs)
6.  src/app/api/auth/register/route.ts (Registration endpoint)
7.  src/app/api/auth/login/route.ts (Login endpoint)
8.  src/app/auth/sign-in/page.tsx (Login UI)
9.  src/app/auth/role-select/page.tsx (Post-signup role assignment)

PHASE 2: PATIENT'S REQUEST (THE "ASK")
Goal: A patient can describe their medical concern.
10. db/tables/cases.sql (Case schema: status, title, description)
11. src/lib/patients.ts (Logic to create and fetch patient cases)
12. src/app/api/patients/cases/route.ts (API for case operations)
13. src/app/patient/create-case/page.tsx (Case submission form)
14. src/app/patient/my-cases/page.tsx (List view of patient's requests)

PHASE 3: ASSESSOR'S WORK (THE "REVIEW")
Goal: A medical contributor can provide an opinion.
15. db/tables/responses.sql (Schema for medical second opinions)
16. src/lib/assessors.ts (Logic for assessors to view feed and respond)
17. src/app/api/assessors/cases/route.ts (API to list open cases for reviewers)
18. src/app/api/assessors/responses/route.ts (API to submit an opinion)
19. src/app/assessor/dashboard/page.tsx (Feed of available cases)
20. src/app/assessor/case-details/page.tsx (Read-only view of patient data)
21. src/app/assessor/submit-response/page.tsx (The medical opinion editor)

PHASE 4: CLOSING THE LOOP (THE "DEMO MILESTONE")
Goal: The patient sees the result. THE APP IS NOW DEMO-READY.
22. src/app/patient/case-details/page.tsx (Detailed view with assessor's response)
23. src/components/status-badge.tsx (Visual indicator: Pending / In Review / Completed)
24. src/app/patient/dashboard/page.tsx (Overview of patient activity)

PHASE 5: POLISH & TRUST (THE "PRODUCT")
Goal: Add security, files, and communication.
25. db/tables/case_files.sql (Schema for image/attachment tracking)
26. src/lib/storage.ts (Logic for handling file uploads/S3/Local)
27. src/components/file-upload.tsx (Image upload UI component)
28. src/lib/safeguards.ts (Logic for consent checks and emergency detection)
29. src/app/admin/assessor-verification/page.tsx (Admin UI to approve credentials)
30. db/tables/messages.sql (Schema for follow-up chat)
31. src/lib/messages.ts (Logic for secure messaging between parties)
32. src/app/api/messages/threads/route.ts (Messaging API)
33. src/app/patient/messages/page.tsx (Patient chat interface)
34. src/app/assessor/messages/page.tsx (Assessor chat interface)
35. src/app/shared/components/navbar.tsx (Final navigation overhaul)
